**Testing tool** is the Jira test-management app and its Rovo agent (Marketplace name: **AI Testing Tool**). Manage requirements, TestCases, and cycles on the project page; ask **Testing Agent** to draft TestCases — it writes only after you confirm.

## User guide

Open the [user guide](./docs/) to install, make a project TMS-ready, design cases, run cycles, and publish CI results into Jira.
